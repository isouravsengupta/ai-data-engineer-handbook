🚀 Google DeepMind Daily Paper Skimming Protocol

As a DeepMind Research Engineer, staying ahead of rapid advancements is your greatest advantage. Do not fall into tutorial hell. Instead, dedicate **30 minutes every single morning** to building your personal reference graph.

1. The Abstract Pass (10m)

Read the Title & Abstract. Classify the paper immediately: Is it a Dataset paper, an Incremental tweak, or a Foundational shift?

2. The Visual Setup Pass (10m)

Study the main architecture diagrams and the experimental results tables. Focus on the scaling limits and system parameters.

3. Trace & Classify (10m)

Read the conclusion and check the Related Work section. Add the paper as a node in your mental system engineering graph.
