# Networking Strategy

Build relationships that lead to referrals, mentorship, and interview opportunities.

## Target Network Mix

- 40% engineers (data, ML, platform)
- 30% hiring managers and tech leads
- 20% recruiters
- 10% founders/researchers/community builders

## Weekly Networking System

- send 10 quality connection requests
- start 5 meaningful conversations
- post 1 technical insight from your current project
- engage with 10 relevant posts thoughtfully
- do 1 informational call (20-30 min)

## Cold Message Template (Short)

Hi `<Name>`, I am building toward Senior Data/AI Engineering roles and really liked your work on `<topic>`.  
I recently built `<project/result>`. Would love to connect and learn from your journey.

## Informational Call Questions

- what skills separate good from great in this role?
- what projects signal real readiness?
- what common interview mistakes do candidates make?
- what should someone at my stage focus on next 90 days?

## Metrics to Track

- new relevant connections/week
- response rate
- calls booked/month
- referrals generated/quarter
