# Interview Strategy

Technical and behavioral preparation for AI Data Engineer roles.

## Interview Loop Breakdown

- coding (Python + DSA + SQL)
- system design (data platform and AI systems)
- ML/LLM fundamentals
- behavioral and leadership

## 6-Week Prep Plan

- Week 1-2: coding and SQL speed
- Week 3-4: data/AI system design
- Week 5: mock interviews and weak-area repair
- Week 6: company-specific preparation and polish

## Daily Prep (120-150 min)

- 60 min coding (1-2 medium or 1 hard)
- 30 min SQL/system design question
- 20 min behavioral STAR story rehearsal
- 20 min revision and mistakes log

## Must-Have Story Bank (STAR)

Prepare at least 8 stories:

- ownership under ambiguity
- debugging production incident
- performance/cost optimization
- cross-team conflict resolution
- delivery under tight deadline
- failed experiment and learning
- mentoring and leadership
- architecture trade-off decision
