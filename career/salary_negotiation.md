# Salary Negotiation

Negotiate with preparation, evidence, and calm execution.

## Negotiation Principles

- never negotiate without market data
- discuss total compensation, not only base salary
- communicate value with concrete impact stories
- stay collaborative, not adversarial

## Preparation Checklist

- target role and level benchmarked
- compensation data from 3-5 reliable sources
- your impact narrative documented
- minimum acceptable package defined
- ideal package and walk-away criteria defined

## Offer Conversation Framework

1. Express excitement for the role
2. Reconfirm fit and expected impact
3. Present market-backed compensation range
4. Ask if flexibility exists across base/equity/sign-on
5. Pause and let recruiter respond

## Negotiation Script (Short)

I am excited about the role and confident I can create strong impact in your data and AI platform.  
Based on market benchmarks and my experience in `<area>`, I was targeting a total package in the `<range>` range.  
Is there flexibility to align the offer accordingly?

## Mistakes to Avoid

- negotiating emotionally
- accepting first offer immediately without review
- ignoring equity vesting and refresh details
- sharing an unrealistically high number without evidence
