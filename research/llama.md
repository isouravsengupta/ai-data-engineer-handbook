# LLaMA

## Why It Matters

LLaMA-series models improved open model accessibility and enabled strong local/self-hosted LLM experimentation.

## Core Ideas

- decoder-only Transformer architecture
- training/data recipe optimization
- variants designed for efficient deployment

## Engineering Takeaways

- suitable for private deployment and customization
- requires careful quantization/inference strategy
- model selection depends on quality, latency, and hardware budget
