# Transformer Paper (Attention Is All You Need)

Original: Vaswani et al., 2017.

## Why It Matters

The Transformer replaced recurrence with attention, enabling massive parallelization and better scaling for language tasks.

## Core Ideas

- self-attention for token-to-token interaction
- multi-head attention for diverse representation subspaces
- positional encoding for sequence order
- feed-forward layers for non-linear transformation

## Engineering Impact

- unlocked LLM-era scaling
- improved training throughput on GPUs/TPUs
- formed the base architecture for modern language models

## Practical Takeaway

When building AI systems, understanding attention and sequence modeling helps with model behavior, context management, and inference optimization decisions.
