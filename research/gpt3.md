# GPT-3

## Why It Matters

GPT-3 demonstrated few-shot and zero-shot capabilities at scale, shifting NLP toward prompt-driven usage.

## Core Ideas

- large autoregressive Transformer decoder
- in-context learning via prompts
- scale-driven capability emergence

## Engineering Takeaways

- prompt design becomes part of system design
- token/context window constraints impact architecture
- inference cost and latency must be actively managed
