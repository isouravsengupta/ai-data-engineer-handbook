# BERT

## Why It Matters

BERT introduced deep bidirectional pretraining, significantly improving understanding-heavy NLP tasks.

## Core Ideas

- masked language modeling (MLM)
- next sentence prediction (original setup)
- bidirectional context representation

## Engineering Takeaways

- strong encoder for retrieval/ranking/classification tasks
- useful for semantic search and domain adaptation
- fine-tuning gives strong performance with moderate data
